Badges on your profile page are made of <Term word="html">HTML</Term>. Let's build a simple one.

HTML marks up text with **tags** in angle brackets. `<h1>` starts a main heading and `</h1>` (with a slash) ends it. `<p>` and `</p>` wrap a paragraph.

Build a card that shows the **First Step** badge as a heading, with "Finish your first unit." as a paragraph below it. Watch the live preview as you type.
