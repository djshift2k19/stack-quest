<Term word="css">CSS</Term> controls how things look. A CSS **rule** has a **selector** (which things to style) and **properties** (how to style them):

```css
.badge {
  color: green;
}
```

`.badge` selects everything with `class="badge"`. Give the badge card a **gold** background colour and **16px** of padding (space between the edge and the text).
