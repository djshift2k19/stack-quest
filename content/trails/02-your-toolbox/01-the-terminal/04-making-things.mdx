Time to make things.

- `mkdir name` **makes** a folder (directory).
- `touch name` makes an **empty file**.
- `echo "text"` prints text. Add `> file` to put the text **into a file** instead.
- `cat file` shows what's **in** a file.

**Naming tip:** avoid spaces in file and folder names. Use dashes (`my-first-site`). In the terminal, a space separates words, so `mkdir my site` would make **two** folders: `my` and `site`.
