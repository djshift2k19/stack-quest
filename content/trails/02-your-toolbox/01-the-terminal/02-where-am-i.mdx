Two commands you'll use constantly:

- `pwd` prints the <Term word="working-directory">working directory</Term>, the folder the terminal is "standing in".
- `ls` lists what's in a folder.

Click in the black terminal below, type each command and press **Enter**. Your tasks are listed under the terminal. If you make a typo, the error translator explains what happened, so just try again.

**Tip:** press the **↑ arrow key** to bring back your last command.
