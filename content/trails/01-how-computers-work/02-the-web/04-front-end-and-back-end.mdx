<Analogy title="A bank branch">
At a bank you see the **front**: the counter, the forms, the tellers and the ATM screen. Behind the wall is the **back**: the vault, the records and the systems that actually move money. Customers only touch the front, but the bank can't work without the back.
</Analogy>

## The front end

The <Term word="frontend">front end</Term> is everything that runs on **your** device and that you see and touch: pages, buttons, forms and animations. It's built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, often using a tool called **React** (Trail 5).

In Stack Quest, the progress rings, the code editor and the celebration when you finish a unit are front end.

## The back end

The <Term word="backend">back end</Term> runs on **servers**, out of sight. It:

- stores and fetches data in a <Term word="database">database</Term> (Trail 7)
- checks passwords and who's allowed to do what (Trail 8)
- talks to other services, like sending an email or taking a card payment

In a shopping app, the back end saves your order, checks the item is in stock and charges your card.

## Full stack

The "stack" is all the layers piled together: front end, back end and database. A <Term word="full-stack">full-stack developer</Term> can work on every layer, so they can build a whole app alone or understand any part of a team's work.

That's the goal of Stack Quest:

- **Trails 3–5:** front end (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React)
- **Trails 6–8:** back end (servers, databases, logins)
- **Trails 9–11:** making it reliable, putting it online and using AI well

<KeyIdea>
The **front end** is what runs in your browser. The **back end** runs on servers and looks after data and rules. A **full-stack** developer builds both.
</KeyIdea>
