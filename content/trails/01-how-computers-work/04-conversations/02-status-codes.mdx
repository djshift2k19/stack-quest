<Analogy title="Traffic lights with extra detail">
Green, amber, red: you know instantly how things stand. A <Term word="status-code">status code</Term> does the same for every response, and its **first digit** tells you most of the story.
</Analogy>

## The five families

- **1xx**: "Hold on…" (rare, you can ignore these)
- **2xx**: ✅ **it worked**
- **3xx**: ↪️ **look somewhere else** (redirects)
- **4xx**: 🙋 **the client made a mistake**: the request was wrong
- **5xx**: 🔥 **the server made a mistake**: it broke while trying

The 4xx/5xx split is gold when debugging: it tells you whose side the problem is on.

## The ones you'll meet every week

- **200 OK**: here's what you asked for.
- **201 Created**: your new thing was made (after a POST).
- **301 Moved Permanently**: this lives at a new address now, and the browser follows automatically.
- **400 Bad Request**: what you sent doesn't make sense (for example, a missing field).
- **401 Unauthorized**: please log in first.
- **403 Forbidden**: you're logged in, but you're not allowed to do this.
- **404 Not Found**: there's nothing at this address. Try `/trails/typo` in Stack Quest to see our friendly 404 page!
- **500 Internal Server Error**: the server's code crashed. It's not your fault as the visitor.

You already met 200 and 404 in the "A tiny XP API" Boss Challenge in the How This App Works trail.

<KeyIdea>
**2xx** = success, **3xx** = go elsewhere, **4xx** = the request was wrong (client's side), **5xx** = the server broke (server's side).
</KeyIdea>
