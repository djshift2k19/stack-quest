  - Recall the key ideas from this module.
questions:
  - prompt: You tap "Send" on a WhatsApp message. Which part is the input?
    options:
      - text: Your finger tapping the screen and typing the words
        correct: true
        why: Yes. Input is information going into the computer.
      - text: The message appearing on your friend's phone
        why: That's output, on your friend's phone.
      - text: The phone packaging up your message
        why: That's processing, the work in the middle.
    hint: Input means "information going in".
  - prompt: You rename holiday.jpg to holiday.txt. What happens to the photo inside?
    options:
      - text: Nothing changes inside. The computer just gets confused about which program should open it.
        correct: true
        why: Right. The extension is a label, not the contents.
      - text: It turns into text
        why: Renaming only changes the label. The photo data is still there, unchanged.
      - text: The photo is deleted
        why: Nothing is deleted. Change the name back and it opens normally again.
  - prompt: "Which of these is a program, rather than data?"
    options:
      - text: Safari
        correct: true
        why: Safari is a set of instructions your computer runs, so it's a program.
      - text: cv.pdf
        why: That's data, a document that a program opens.
      - text: Your Stack Quest XP
        why: That's data that the Stack Quest program works out and stores.
    hint: Remember the recipe (program) and the ingredients (data).
